Before you pick Faster
In the best study we have, AI's quality gain beat its speed gain: about 40% higher quality against 25% faster, on work inside what AI does well (Dell'Acqua et al., 2023). Real-world time savings for teachers are small, under 3% of working hours (Humlum and Vestergaard, 2025). And on work outside what AI does well, people were 19 points more likely to get it wrong. Pick the tag that's true for you, and keep your own judgment in the loop.
